# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Standards

**Expected behavior:**
- Professional and respectful communication
- Constructive feedback and collaboration
- Focus on AWS security knowledge sharing

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Spam or self-promotion without value
- Sharing malicious content or exploits without responsible disclosure

## Enforcement

Violations may result in removal of contributions or blocking from the repository.

## Contact

Report issues via GitHub Issues or contact the maintainer directly.
